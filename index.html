About Fernwood

Fifteen years of gentle, advanced care

Fernwood Animal Hospital opened in 2011 with a simple belief: outstanding medicine and genuine warmth aren't a trade-off. Today our AAHA-accredited hospital blends digital diagnostics, in-house surgery and dentistry, and a fear-free approach with the kind of unhurried attention every pet — and every pet parent — deserves.

Mission

Deliver compassionate, evidence-based care that keeps pets healthy at every life stage.

Vision

Be the most trusted animal hospital in the region — for pets, and the people who love them.

Values

Empathy first, honest communication, and never rushing a decision that matters.

Emergency & After-Hours Care

If your pet is in crisis, call us immediately — day or night. Our team will guide you through what to do before you arrive.

(555) 019-9911
After-hours & holidays: 24/7 emergency line staffed

Before You Arrive

  1. Call ahead so our team can prepare for your pet's condition.
  2. Keep your pet warm, calm, and as still as safely possible.
  3. Do not give food, water, or medication unless instructed.
  4. Bring any medications or toxins your pet may have ingested.
